## Tips for Description Optimization

1. **Front-load important keywords**: First 250 characters are most important (shown before "Read more")
2. **Use emojis sparingly**: They catch attention but don't overdo it
3. **Break into scannable sections**: Use headers and bullet points
4. **Include social proof**: Add this after you get reviews/downloads
5. **Call to action**: Tell users explicitly what to do next
6. **Update regularly**: Refresh description when you add features
